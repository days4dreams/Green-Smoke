It’s Sunday afternoon. The house is quiet, the coffee’s on, and I’ve got an hour or two at my desk. Some people might call that working the weekend. I call it a privilege.

Because I’m choosing to be here. And come Monday, there’s no traffic to fight to get my bum at a desk for a set time.

It isn’t all roses, but it’s pretty sweet.

#### Work that fits ####

Since becoming a mum, the quiet hours have become precious. A Tuesday evening once bedtime’s done, a hot chocolate (always 'Options') that's still warm, and a bit of headspace to think.

When you run your own business, work doesn’t have to live between nine and five. It happens in the gaps - an early morning, late night, weekend afternoon. Work fits around family, rather than the other way round. That’s the bit I wouldn’t swap for anything.

#### If you’re juggling too ####

If you run a small business, you’ll know the feeling. Emails sent at 10pm, a to-do list that never quite ends (!), and the sense that you’re walking a tightrope between it all.
