I design and create in a way that looks good and does good.

View recent work

Whether you’re building a brand, in search of creative support, or have a digital project to deliver, I'm here to help. Together, we can design products and services that are sustainably made, ethically sourced, accessible and inclusive.

I believe in doing things - big or small - that make the world a bit of a better place, and support others to do the same. I belong to a community who are a force for good. Who reduce, re-use, repair, recover and recycle.

I help others to stop wasting time, money and resources. I ask ‘Could it be made better? More beautiful? For longer? For less cost to the planet and the customer?’

Over a decade of design industry experience

I'm multidisciplinary designer. That's a fancy way of saying I work across various kinds of design. Here's a summary of the kind of work that I do:

WEB DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT

From getting a new business their first site up and running, to supporting an established brand with a new look or piece of content. I'll help out with everything from picking out a domain name (site address) to publishing your own blog post, or integrating a booking platform.

USER EXPERIENCE (UX) DESIGN

Making improvements to your website or app not only makes for happier customers, but an improved set of performance metrics, too. I can help to alleviate pain points of your visitors, speed up the journey to your all-important end goals and make your site sustainable, at the same time.

DIGITAL DESIGN

Whether a product brochure, email campaign or social media assets, I'm on hand to help you work out what tells your story best, and make it easy for you to share.

PRINT DESIGN

From business cards to bespoke stationery, magazine publications to marketing material, I'm happy to help.

BRANDING AND CONSULTANCY

No matter if you're a brand-new business, or an established firm, your brand is in safe hands. From understanding your target market to point-of-sale design materials, I have the understanding and experience to support.
